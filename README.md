# silas sao is an engineer turned designer.

i'm head of design at [supabase](https://supabase.com).

previously, i was a design director for data and ai at [ibm](https://ibm.com), working on agentic experiences and design systems. i led the team through ibm's acquisition of [datastax](https://datastax.com), where i built product experience, spanning brand and product design, technical content, and product analytics.

before that, i led design for apm, infrastructure, traces, and logs at [new relic](https://newrelic.com).

i was born in hawaii and now live in the pacific northwest. i'm completely self-taught, and i spend my time experimenting with emerging technologies and exploring how they shape human behavior.
